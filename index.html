Vision

Beyond Legacy Command

The defense industry builds for the fight in front of it. Programs get scoped to a battlespace, a budget cycle, a slot in someone else’s command stack. We plan further out.

Every system we field runs on its own and talks to the others. At volume they map the fight. That picture is not a feed into someone else’s command software. It is what command runs on.

The fight is getting faster and denser than people can run by hand. We are building systems that can keep up across the whole battlespace.
