Vision

Beyond Legacy Command

The defense industry optimizes for the fight in front of it. Programs are scoped to a battlespace, a budget cycle, an integration slot in someone else’s command stack. Theron takes a longer view.

Every physical system we field is built to operate fully autonomously and to connect. Deployed at volume, these platforms form a networked awareness of the operational environment — not as inputs to legacy command software, but as the substrate from which command itself can be reconceived.

Conflict is moving toward a pace and complexity that human-mediated control cannot match. We are building the infrastructure for what comes after: autonomous coordination across the full depth of the battlespace, giving allied nations defensive capability that operates at the speed the threat demands.

About

Real Hardware, Built Today

Theron is a San Francisco–based defense technology company, founded in 2026. We are focused on building real physical products — systems that exist in the field, not on a slide deck.

Our roots are in hard tech: mechanical design, electronics, and production engineering. We pair that with deep expertise in controls software and AI — from real-time system control through perception and autonomous decision-making. We stay close to operators, move fast, and measure success by what ships.
